# Figlo identity

The icon is an original geometric lowercase f. Its five colors recall design
tools; the tilted square opening hints at building in Roblox. It does not use
either platform's logo. Use `icon.svg` at 32 pixels or larger with its dark tile
and clear space of at least one eighth of its width.

Colors: orange `#F24E1E`, coral `#FF7262`, violet `#A259FF`, blue `#1ABCFE`,
green `#0ACF83`, background `#141820`. The wordmark is simply **Figlo**, with
an uppercase F, in the surrounding interface's sans-serif font. Tagline:
**Figma frames. Roblox interfaces.**

This is the first alpha identity. The original SVG is covered by the repository's
MIT license. Do not imply affiliation or endorsement by Figma or Roblox.
